About

Made by a dice setter, for dice setters.

Dice Control Pro is built by JohnBobApps, an independent studio run by one developer who throws dice himself. The goal is simple: honest tools that do one job well, cost one fair price, and respect your privacy.

Anyone who practices a controlled throw wants to know one thing: is it working? Pencil-and-paper tallies get lost, and spreadsheets are a chore at the rig. Dice Control Pro counts every roll, checks it against random dice, and shows what your throw is worth on every bet.

Part of the JohnBobApps family

Dice Control Pro joins Casino Bankroll Pro, the session and bankroll tracker for table games and slots, and Bankroll Pro Poker for cash games and tournaments. Same privacy-first approach, built around the way dice setters practice and play.
