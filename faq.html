SRR and your throw

What is SRR?

SRR is your sevens-to-rolls ratio: total rolls divided by the number of sevens. Random dice throw a seven on 6 of the 36 combinations, an SRR of 6.0. An SRR above 6.0 means you are throwing fewer sevens than random.

How many rolls before I can trust my SRR?

A few hundred rolls can swing a lot by luck. The app's luck check shows how often random dice would match your number, and it labels small samples. Treat any figure from fewer than 5,000 rolls with a set as a rough guide.

Should I log dice faces or just totals?

Faces, whenever you can. Faces let the app see whether your dice stayed on axis, power the axis and pitch stats, and decide hardway bets. Totals are faster but count toward SRR only.

Which dice sets does it support?

Hardway (with 3, 2, 4 or 5 on top), 3V, 2V, All Sevens, Yo, Straight Sixes and Crossed Sixes are built in, and you can build any set of your own. Each set keeps its own SRR and stats.

The betting table

Is the betting table real money?

No. The betting table uses a play-money bankroll so you can practice your betting with real Las Vegas payouts. Nothing is wagered, won or lost.

Which bets and payouts does it use?

Pass line and come with true-odds odds up to 3-4-5x (or your own limit), place bets at 9 to 5, 7 to 5 and 7 to 6, hard 6 and 8 at 9 to 1, hard 4 and 10 at 7 to 1, the field with double on 2 and triple on 12, All Tall All Small, and a pass line bet for the dealers. Payouts round down to the dollar like a real table.

Does it handle off and on?

Yes. When a come bet on a number wins while you have a new come bet in the come box, the bets stay put and you're paid the win, just like at the table.

Your data

Where are my rolls stored?

Only on your device. There is no account and nothing is sent to any server, so nobody else sees your rolls unless you share them.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Use Download backup on the Sessions screen, move the file to your new phone, then use Restore from a backup file.

Can I get my rolls into a spreadsheet?

Yes. Download rolls as CSV exports every roll from every session, with its set, how the dice landed and your notes. It opens in Excel or Google Sheets.
