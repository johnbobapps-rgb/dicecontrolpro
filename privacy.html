Privacy Policy

Your data stays yours.

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JohnBobApps LLC ("we," "us") handles information in the Dice Control Pro mobile app (the "App") and on dicecontrolpro.com (the "Website").

The short version

The App does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal information. Everything you enter is stored only on your device.

Information you enter in the App

The rolls you log, your dice sets, session notes, settings and the play-money betting table are saved in local storage on your device. We do not have access to this information, and it is never sent to our servers or any third party.

Exports and backups

If you export your rolls as a CSV file or download a backup, the file is created on your device. Where it goes after that, such as email or cloud storage, is up to you and is governed by the service you choose.

No accounts, ads or tracking

  • The App does not require an account or login.
  • The App contains no advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • The App does not include third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Purchases

The App is free to download and try. The full version is unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your payment card details or any personal information about your purchase.

No real-money gambling

The App's betting table is a simulation that uses play money only. No money is wagered, won or lost, and the App does not connect to any gambling service.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you control it completely. You can erase all rolls and sessions in the App's Options, or delete the App or clear its data in your device settings. We cannot recover deleted data.

The Website

The Website does not ask for personal information. To understand how many people visit and which pages they read, it uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies and collects information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device type and how you found the site. This information is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can block these cookies in your browser settings or with Google's opt-out browser add-on.

The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information such as IP addresses for security purposes under GitHub's own privacy policy.

None of this applies to the App. The App itself contains no analytics and collects nothing.

Children

The App is intended for adults of legal gambling age in their jurisdiction. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and revise the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@johnbobapps.com.
